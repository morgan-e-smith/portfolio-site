# Frequently asked questions about Morgan

## Role and fit

**What kind of role is Morgan looking for?** A Director or Senior Manager role leading AI adoption in marketing, where the job is turning AI strategy into how a whole function works. New York area or remote.

**What makes her different from other AI transformation candidates?** She does all three parts of the job: sets the strategy, builds or co-builds the tools, and drives the change until people actually use them. Most candidates do one or two. In her current role she leads AI adoption for a 2,000-person marketing function, with 100% of the org onboarded to the enterprise AI platform and 14 agents in production with engineering.

**What is her most significant accomplishment?** Three stand out. Leading AI adoption across a 2,000-person marketing function: 100% of the org onboarded to the enterprise AI platform and 14 agents in production with engineering. Creating an AI recognition program that got colleagues experimenting and sharing what they built, with 85 submissions in four weeks. And before that, running a global company website as a product through a complete redesign across 50 countries: owning the strategy, roadmap, and backlog, shaping the front-end experience with UX and design teams, and redesigning the operations behind it. Engagement rose 24% and bounce rate fell 25%.

**What industries has she worked in?** B2B enterprise marketing in professional services and consulting, plus early agency experience. Eight-plus years total.

**Why should we hire her?** Morgan commits fully and isn't afraid to get her hands dirty. She's curious by default and learns enough of every role on a project to work well with the people in it: on the company website, she taught herself Figma, AEM, and the language of design so she could be a real partner to UX and product teams. Today she's leading her marketing function's push to build its own HTML tools and assets.

## How Morgan works

**How does she work with engineering teams?** She prototypes quickly herself, then partners with product and engineering to scale what works. She brings the use case, the requirements, and the user testing; they bring the architecture. She has also tested a beta platform alongside its product manager, documenting issues with reproduction steps and working through the fixes together.

**Has she managed people?** Mostly through influence: matrixed teams, a champion network she founded, and 30 digital strategists she trained and led as a functional group through a site-wide design system rollout. She has also managed writers directly on the company website program.

**How does she use AI in her own work?** As a build and drafting partner, never the decision-maker. She owns strategy, stakeholder and legal decisions, and final sign-off; AI accelerates drafts, code, data cleanup, and consistency checks. She verifies every number before anything goes out.

**Can she build things herself?** Yes, at the prototype level: custom HTML apps, Copilot agents, and this site, built with Claude Code. For production systems, she partners with engineering.

**What is her working style?** Morgan works hard and is at her best on problems nobody has solved yet. She took on an AI transformation role in a field where no one knows what the future of AI in marketing looks like, and made it work through her own initiative. She's direct, highly organized, and energized by getting to help shape where AI and marketing go next.

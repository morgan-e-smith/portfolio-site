Cards present one case study or essay: pillar, proof point or status, title, a one-line summary, and a call to action.

- Surface `paper-raised`, 1px `line` border, `radius-md`, `space-3` padding. Hover turns the border `turquoise-ink`. No shadows, no colored left borders.
- Top row: a pillar tag on the left; on the right either a stat ("85 submissions") or a status tag ("In progress"), never both.
- Title in `card-title` (Fraunces 22/28). Summary in `body-s`, `ink-muted`, outcome first, two sentences at most.
- The whole card is one link. Lay cards out one column on mobile, three across on desktop with `space-3` gutters.
